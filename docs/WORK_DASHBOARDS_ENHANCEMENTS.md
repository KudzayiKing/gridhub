# Work dashboard enhancements

Project Management, Habit Tracker, and Social Content Planner now lead with a task/check-in/content status visualization on the left and up to six KPIs in one card on the right.

- Project completion uses current task statuses and explicit completion dates only.
- Habit summaries use configured schedules and recorded check-ins. Future dates are not treated as missed.
- Content status and dates are user-entered. A scheduled post remains distinct from a published post, and engagement is shown only from manually recorded metrics.

Existing Kanban, habit calendar/grid, and editorial management views remain available below the dashboard summary.

Limitations: historical project completion is not reconstructed from today's status; workload by team member is unavailable in the solo task model; no social platform metrics or publishing integration is connected.
