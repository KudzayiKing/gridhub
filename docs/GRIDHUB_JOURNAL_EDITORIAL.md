# Journal editorial workflow

Article statuses: idea → researching → brief_ready → drafting → draft → quality_review → awaiting_approval → approved → scheduled → published. `needs_revision` and `archived` are available review outcomes. Article body edits create append-only revisions and invalidate approval. Metadata edits also return the working article to draft. A previously published revision remains public while a later revision is being reviewed.

Before editorial review, automated checks require a title, body, primary keyword, SEO title and description, researched intent, at least one editor-verified source, and a related calculator or tool. The editor must then complete the nine-item human checklist on the exact current revision. The server rejects approval unless every checklist item is confirmed. Research and drafting routes cannot approve content.

The editor supports safe Markdown, an in-admin rendered preview, source records, revision restore as a new revision, Markdown import/export, and brief JSON export. HTML is not executed. Images can be set as a featured image URL with alt text; no image-upload or licensing service is connected. Editors remain responsible for image rights and source verification.

High-impact financial, tax, legal, and health claims require stronger human review. The software does not certify regulatory accuracy or source quality merely because an editor marked a source verified. No article is published automatically from research or generation.
