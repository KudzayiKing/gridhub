## LinkedIn — educational post

When paying off several debts, where should the extra payment go?

The debt snowball targets the smallest balance first. The debt avalanche targets the highest APR first. Keep making minimum payments on every account either way.

If your total payment and balances are otherwise comparable, the avalanche generally directs more money away from interest. The snowball can help you reach an earlier payoff milestone. The strategy you can keep following matters.
